# D3-03 Claims check: abroad, protocols, rules (agent: Sonnet 5.5, 2026-11-13)

Notes checked: D1-05 Players abroad and failures; D1-06 Buildable by noon; D2-04 Regulation, law and who pays; D2-06 Funded abroad; new facts D2-01-F1 to F16; posture and photo facts in D2-02. All sources opened 2026-11-13. Local copies of the PDFs were text-extracted; the Gujarat sheet was also rendered as an image to read the symbols.

## Verdict
85 checks (rows; several rows group related facts): 63 CONFIRMED, 13 CORRECTED, 2 CONTESTED, 7 NOT FOUND. The numbers being typed into software are right: Gujarat steps, doses, 30-day interval and thresholds; Punjab, Maharashtra and the 70/75/77, 76/81/82 and 159,292 of 281,209 figures; Nigeria steps. What was incomplete: the Gujarat pregnancy box also bars ACE inhibitors, ARBs, thiazides and statins for women who may become pregnant, the lab-check footnote says withdraw telmisartan and add chlorthalidone if no specialist is reachable, and the population is adults over 30. Nigeria's "still high" is 140/90 and its pregnancy rule (no losartan; refer if pregnant) is on the sheet. Other corrections: ADA wording is the 2025 figure; telemedicine clause 5.4 is stronger than the law-firm paraphrase and Annexure 1 names hypertension examples; Teladoc's $13.4B is not a Livongo charge; AAMOS-00 has 1,099 peak-flow days, not 2,054; Biobeat's page names its leads; Aktiia's co-leads and Biolinq's 2024 round are not on the pages cited.

Never say: "ADA 2026 says 2 units every 3 days"; "Teladoc wrote down Livongo by $13.4B"; "Pear had $130M of liabilities" or "$1,195 a course" (unopened); "Cadence's AI resolves 55% of dose changes" (it is 55% of alerts); "Cadence is valued at $1.23B" or "Nourish at $1.75B"; "Simple has no step logic anywhere" (only its protocol model); "omblepy is open-source licensed" (no licence found); "the control rates are home readings" (clinic readings, returners only); "DPDP consent duties start 12 May 2027" without the date caveat.
