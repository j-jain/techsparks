## Problem 1: Combustible-dust and reactive-chemical blasts in plants with paper-only safety (Sigachi, Deesa)

- Track: Frontier Hardware & Climate (also AI & Systems)
- Who suffers: workers (mostly migrant, contract) in pharma-excipient, chemical, powder, firecracker and similar units; Telangana, Gujarat, Rajasthan, Maharashtra, MP, Chhattisgarh. Deaths cluster in a handful of states: top 5 states = 425 of 660 factory-accident deaths in 2024 (about 64%).
- How they cope today: periodic Factories Department inspection, ISO paperwork, nothing continuous on the shop floor. The Sigachi expert panel found inspection reports 2019-2024 "did not reflect the factual violations" (snippet, Deccan Herald/Deccan Chronicle). Compensation after the fact (Rs 2 lakh PMNRF, Rs 4 lakh Gujarat CM fund after Deesa).
- Public proof (tier B; hard number strong, no social engagement figure):
  - https://telanganatoday.com/sigachi-blast-expert-panel-finds-multiple-violations-holds-management-responsible — 23 Nov 2025 — news — (fetched) expert committee: 54 dead; epicentre = packing area where microcrystalline-cellulose dust formed a cloud, ignited near a bag-sealing machine; management knew MCC was combustible and did nothing to limit dust; no heat/smoke detection, sprinklers or hydrant; no helmets, shoes, respirators or gloves; claimed ISO 14001/45001 it did not hold; production up 30% in March 2025 in a 1994 building.
  - https://www.deccanchronicle.com/southern-states/telangana/sigachi-blast-total-failure-on-safety-fake-quality-claims-and-lies-1918900 — undated (post Nov 2025) — news — (snippet) headline "Total Failure on Safety, Fake Quality Claims, and Lies".

  - Social engagement (Reddit/YouTube): NOT REACHABLE this session.
- Hard number: NCRB "Factory/machine accidents" 2024 = 742 accidents, 660 deaths (about 89 deaths per 100 accidents); Gujarat 121, Rajasthan 95, Maharashtra 87, MP 70, Chhattisgarh 52; 2010 was 1,586 accidents and 1,043 deaths — https://insights.dataful.in/articles/factory-accidents-are-falling-but-deaths-remain-high — 12 Jun 2026 — (fetched). Older DGFASLI via RTI: average 1,109 deaths and 4,000+ injuries a year in registered factories 2017-2020, and only 14 people jailed under the Factories Act 2018-2020; experts say the figures undercount, informal sector (about 90% of workers) not covered — https://janataweekly.org/articles/three-workers-die-every-day-in-indian-factories-govt-data-show — IndiaSpend piece dated 18 Feb 2023 (fetched).

- Overnight feasibility (1-5): 4. Sensor node, threshold alarm, dashboard and a talc/cornstarch-in-a-sealed-box dust demo (low concentration only, no ignition tests) are doable; real explosion-limit calibration is not.

Willingness to pay looks weak where enforcement is weak (14 jailed in 3 years); a more likely payer is an insurer or an auditing customer. That last point is my inference.
- Risks / regulation: Factories Act, state fire services acts; ATEX-style certification for anything sold into hazardous zones; liability if the alarm fails.

## Problem 2: Silicosis and respirable dust in stone, quarry, crusher, ceramic and foundry units

- Track: Bio & Health (also Frontier Hardware)
- Who suffers: quarry, stone-carving, crusher, sandstone and quartz workers; Rajasthan (Kotputli-Behror, Jodhpur sandstone), Gujarat (Godhra/Panchmahal), Jharkhand ramming-mass units, Odisha artisans working in Rajasthan. Often migrant, informal, no employer record.
- How they cope today: cloth or paper masks, nothing measured. Diagnosis needs repeated trips to CHC or district hospital; certified patients get Rs 5,000/month and free medicines in Rajasthan; oxygen cylinders at about Rs 1,000 each, one 32-year-old used 12 a day before dying (PTI/DT Next).
- Public proof (tier B; hard number strong, no engagement numbers):
  - https://www.dtnext.in/news/national/death-by-breath-how-mining-dust-in-aravallis-is-killing-residents — 20 Sep 2026 — PTI via DT Next — (fetched) Rajasthan portal: 23,436 certified silicosis cases incl. 6,876 deaths; a patient's X-ray registration cancelled three times in three years with no reviewer known; village of Syalodra says 50+ deaths; NGT banned blasting within 500 m of Jodhpura-Mohanpura.
  - https://caravanmagazine.in/health/silicosis-india-silica-dust-killing-workers — 1 Sep 2024 — Caravan (Pulitzer Center-backed) — (fetched, only the opening) quarry worker with about two decades of silica exposure developed silicosis in his mid-thirties.
  - https://www.theindiaforum.in/amp/economy/estimating-worker-population-exposed-silica-dust-india — 11 Mar 2026 — (fetched) PLFS-based estimate: 55.9 million exposed workers in 2024 (3.99% of population), 94% in construction; about 1.99 million outside construction; GBD 2023: 4,467 deaths; Indian mine standard 100 ug/m3 respirable silica; in one 2020 study 30% of air samples exceeded the standard.
  - https://business-humanrights.org/en/india-238-deaths-later-silicosis-is-still-the-killer-in-godhra’s-quarries — undated — (snippet) Supreme Court ordered compensation for 238 workers who died of silicosis in Godhra quartz/stone crushing units.
  - https://www.downtoearth.org.in/amp/story/health/silicosis-is-a-silent-epidemic — undated — (snippet) "silent epidemic".
  - https://www.pbs.org/newshour/show/dusty-mining-conditions-trap-indian-workers-with-deadly-lung-disease — undated — (snippet) foreign broadcaster documenting Indian mines.
  - YouTube (titles from search hits; view counts NOT AVAILABLE): "Silicosis Patients को इसलिए नहीं मिल रहा पैसा, सच आया सामने" https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VKTisjYjYr0 ; "#Rajasthan में #Silicosis बीमारी से मरते पत्थर खदानों के मज़दूर, सरकारी योजनाएं हैं कोसो दूर" https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VFFWLFPc8lw ; "They Are Dying Just to Earn a Living" https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OH1w_1qA9Nk ; "Workers in India Die Young of Silicosis" https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VW7Xk5VBwao ; "Odisha stone artisans working in Rajasthan are prone to Silicosis" https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=suuBxhA6mYU .
- Hard number: 23,436 certified cases and 6,876 deaths (Rajasthan portal, DT Next 20 Sep 2026, fetched); 4,467 deaths attributed to occupational silica in India in 2023 (GBD, India Forum 11 Mar 2026, fetched). Experts in both say these undercount.
- Who has tried: NOT SEARCHED. The state's answer is the 2019 Rajasthan pneumoconiosis policy and portal (DT Next); no hardware or AI product found in this session.
- Deep-tech wedge (one line): a low-cost respirable-dust logger for each crusher or cutting station (PM sensor calibrated to a gravimetric reference, LoRa/phone upload) that triggers water-spray or exhaust and produces exposure records; bio side: ML triage of chest X-rays against ILO pneumoconiosis grades so certification stops stalling (data access is the blocker).
- Overnight feasibility (1-5): 3. Dust logger + spray trigger + exposure dashboard is 4; the X-ray model is 2 because no open silicosis-labelled dataset was checked.
- Rough market pointer: about 1.99 million non-construction exposed workers (India Forum). The payer is unclear (informal units, state health departments, mining lessees). Down-weight as a venture market; strong as a Bio & Health track story. Pricing: not found.
- Risks / regulation: Rajasthan silicosis policy 2019, NGT orders, mine dust standards (100 ug/m3 silica); a PM2.5 sensor does not measure silica fraction; the buyer may not exist without enforcement.

## Problem 3: Counterfeit bearings (and the spares trade behind them)

- Track: Frontier Hardware & Climate (also AI & Systems)
- Who suffers: workshops, factories, transporters and vehicle owners who buy bearings from unauthorised dealers (Delhi Karol Bagh/Farsh Khana, Madurai, Mumbai, Pune); brands (SKF, NBC/NEI, Schaeffler FAG) lose sales. Fakes fail early and cause line stoppages and safety risk (NBC: "a serious threat to vehicle safety and industry reliability").
- How they cope today: buy from "authorised dealers", check hologram/packaging, QR/app scan (SKF Authenticate, NBC Connect), brand-led police raids. Counterfeiters now run laser-marking machines, so the print on the box and the bearing is faked too.
- Public proof (tier B; hard number from industry and police, no engagement numbers):
  - https://www.autocarpro.in/news-national/skf-india-fights-fake-bearings-menace-raids-25062 — 20 Jun 2017 — (fetched) SKF India MD: Rs 150 crore a year lost to fakes; market about Rs 9,000 crore; "roughly 10 percent is unstructured... half of that is counterfeit"; 22 raids in 2016, 18 in 2015.
  - https://www.nbcbearings.com/nbc-bearings-cracks-down-on-counterfeiters-with-major-raids-in-delhi-and-madurai/ — Jun-Jul 2025 — (fetched, company press release) five raids, goods over Rs 72 lakh, a laser marking machine seized, NBC urges its NBC Connect app.
  - https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/nbc-bearings-intensifies-anti-counterfeiting-drive-with-major-raids-in-delhi-and-bengaluru — undated — (snippet) further NBC raids; Feb 2024 Delhi/Agra raids recovered fakes worth over Rs 28 lakh; a later action seized goods worth over Rs 2 crore with dies and laser-marking machines (snippet summary, page not opened).
  - https://www.lokmattimes.com/national/delhi-police-busts-counterfeit-bearings-racket-recovers-over-5000-fake-units/ — about May 2026 — (snippet; page returned HTTP 403) Delhi Police, Farsh Khana: two workshops raided, 5,067 fake bearings.
  - https://autocarpro.in/news-national/schaeffler-india-conducts-raids-spurious-packaging-dealers-mumbai-8783 — undated — (snippet) 37,000+ counterfeit FAG packaging units seized in Mumbai.
  - Quora (engagement not reachable, HTTP 403): https://www.quora.com/What-are-the-ways-to-identify-a-genuine-SKF-bearing-from-an-inferior-imitation-bearing and https://www.quora.com/How-do-I-identify-original-bearing — answers say check hologram, finish, print (snippet).
  - YouTube (titles only, views unknown): "Fake vs Genuine Bearings. How to Spot a FAKE Bearing?" https://www.youtube.com/shorts/YWKns5opKU4 ; "SKF's customer explains how counterfeit bearings hurt their business" https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R0wOPEHb8Kc ; "SKF Authenticate: How to authenticate SKF products using the scan code" https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoboO2jkBG0 .
- Hard number: SKF India: Rs 150 crore/year lost, market Rs 9,000 crore (Autocar Pro, 20 Jun 2017, fetched). Dated and company-stated. NBC raid seizures Rs 72 lakh in 2025 (fetched, press release).
- Who has tried: brand-owned authentication (SKF Authenticate QR app, NBC Connect app) — alive. Gap: these authenticate the pack or code, and counterfeiters copy packs and marks. No independent third-party physical-authentication startup found (not searched deeply).
- Deep-tech wedge (one line): authenticate the part, not the box: phone macro-photo ML on laser-marking quality, surface texture and cage/seal details, plus a cheap MEMS-mic or accelerometer spin test as an acoustic fingerprint; sell as a verification tool for dealers and workshops.
- Overnight feasibility (1-5): 3. A classifier on genuine vs knock-off photos is easy only if we can get both sets by tomorrow night; buying a few cheap lookalikes and genuine units from a market is the realistic route. Do not claim detection accuracy without that data.
- Rough market pointer: derived from the SKF quote, counterfeit is about 5% of a Rs 9,000 crore market, about Rs 450 crore (my arithmetic on the 2017 quote, not stated in the article). Payers would be the bearing brands (brand-protection budgets) and large fleets. Small market for one SKU class; broader spares (filters, brake parts, electricals) NOT SEARCHED.
- Risks / regulation: need brand cooperation for genuine samples; trademark and consumer law; the buyer is a handful of multinationals and one large Indian maker.

## Problem 4: Unplanned machine stoppages in small plants with no condition monitoring (and bad incoming power)

- Track: AI & Systems (also Frontier Hardware)
- Who suffers: CNC job shops, foundries, plastics and fabrication MSMEs. Owner-voice proof not reached.
- How they cope today: breakdown maintenance, operator walks to find the supervisor and the maintenance man; supplier service engineer. ABB: 19% of Indian industrial businesses still run to failure (snippet).
- Public proof (tier C for owner voices; funding and vendor surveys only):
  - https://new.abb.com/news/detail/108271/abb-survey-reveals-unplanned-downtime-costs-inr-7-million-per-hour — Oct 2023 — (snippet via search summary; page fetch timed out) vendor-commissioned (Sapio Research, July 2023, 3,215 maintenance decision-makers globally): 88% of Indian industrial businesses have an unplanned outage at least monthly (69% globally), about INR 7 million per hour, 19% run to failure. Not MSME-specific and vendor-sponsored.
  - https://www.industrialautomationindia.in/news/presage-insights-raises-5-6-crore-predictive-maintenance-india — 18 Sep 2025 — (fetched) Presage Insights (Mohali), Rs 5.6 crore seed led by YourNest; targets small and mid-size plants "often excluded from advanced Industry 4.0 tools"; claims Rs 50-75 lakh average annual saving (company claim).
  - https://techcrunch.com/2023/05/22/tiger-global-india-infinite-uptime-funding/ — 22 May 2023 — (fetched) Infinite Uptime (Pune): extra $18.85M Series B3 led by Tiger Global; 300+ customers (Vedanta, JSW Steel, Hyundai, Ceat, Adani); claims 11,000+ hours of unplanned downtime saved. A later report says it raised $35M (snippet).
  - https://knnindia.co.in/news/newsdetails/state/poor-power-supply-leads-to-production-loss-as-well-as-damages-machinery-parts-jammu-msmes — 2016 — (snippet) Jammu MSME association: voltage fluctuation causes production loss and damages motors and electrical parts. Old and local.
  - https://cadem.com/industry4-0-reduce-downtime-in-breakdowns/ — 5 Nov 2025 — (fetched) vendor blog; illustrative breakdown timings only, no Indian statistics. A snippet in the same search says an IMTMA national survey flagged CNC downtime as a user difficulty; I did not find the numbers.
  - Power-quality (sags/harmonics) evidence is vendor case studies only (intelliware.in) — no owner-voice or survey found.
- Hard number: ABB's "88% monthly unplanned outages, INR 7 million/hr" (vendor-sponsored, snippet). No government number found for MSME downtime. This is the weakest hard number in the note.
- Who has tried: Infinite Uptime (alive, $18.85M B3 May 2023; large process plants), Presage Insights (alive, Rs 5.6 cr seed Sep 2025, aimed at small plants), Petasense (Bengaluru/US, $1.8M seed 2017, listed active in a third-party database, snippet). Crowded; the unfilled gap is price and install effort for 5-20 machine shops, which I could not confirm with owner statements.
- Deep-tech wedge (one line): a Rs 2-3k clamp-on node (CT current sensor + MEMS accelerometer + voltage-sag logger on an ESP32) that learns each CNC motor's normal signature and texts the owner before a stoppage, also logging sags and harmonics so power faults are separated from machine faults.
- Overnight feasibility (1-5): 5 technically (easy to demo with a motor and a deliberately unbalanced load), but differentiation vs funded players is thin and there is no owner-voice proof; a judge will ask "why not Infinite Uptime".
- Rough market pointer: no MSME count tied to CNC/rotating machines found. Company-quoted saving Rs 50-75 lakh a year (Presage, unverified) is the only price anchor.
- Risks / regulation: low regulatory risk; high sales-cycle risk (owners do not trust retrofits); crowded.

## Problem 5: Electrical short-circuit fires in small, often unlicensed factories

- Track: Frontier Hardware & Climate (also AI & Systems)
- Who suffers: workers sleeping or working in garment, plastics, soap, chemical and firework units; Delhi, Gujarat, Karnataka, Maharashtra. Repeated mass-casualty events.
- How they cope today: fire NOC on paper, extinguishers, occasional MCB checks; many units run with no fire licence (Anaj Mandi building "operating illegally as a factory... lacked a fire license", snippet).
- Public proof (tier B; hard number strong, no engagement numbers):
  - https://insights.dataful.in/articles/nearly-6000-fire-accident-deaths-in-2024-despite-sharp-decline-in-accidents — 5 Jun 2026 — (fetched) NCRB 2024: 5,971 fire accidents, 5,888 deaths; short circuits caused 17.2% of fire deaths; 149 fire incidents in factories.
  - https://ianslive.in/6891-killed-in-7054-fire-accidents-in-2023-nityanand-rai--20260210161804 — Feb 2026 — (snippet) MoS Home to Parliament: 6,891 killed in 7,054 fire accidents in 2023.
  - https://janataweekly.org/articles/three-workers-die-every-day-in-indian-factories-govt-data-show — 18 Feb 2023 — (fetched) Mundka factory fire May 2022: at least 27 killed, unit had no fire department permission and sat in a zone where factories are not allowed.
  - https://www.scmp.com/news/asia/south-asia/article/3041136/least-43-killed-fire-rips-through-delhi-handbag-factory-while — Dec 2019 — (snippet) Anaj Mandi handbag factory: 43 killed, electrical short circuit suspected.
  - https://www.magzter.com/hi/stories/newspaper/The-New-Indian-Express-Tirunelveli/FIVE-KILLED-AFTER-FIRE-BREAKS-OUT-AT-BLURU-PLASTIC-MANUFACTURING-UNIT — Aug 2025 — (snippet) five killed at a plastics unit near Bengaluru, short circuit likely.
  - https://www.arabtimesonline.com/news/2-killed-11-injured-in-indian-textile-factory-fire — Mar 2026 — (snippet) Surat embroidery factory, short circuit then LPG cylinders, 2 dead, 11 hurt.
  - https://ianslive.in/fire-guts-soap-factory-in-gujarat-no-fire-safety-measures-in-place--20250522222625 — 22 May 2025 — (snippet) Deesa soap factory fire, no fire-safety measures.
  - https://deshgujarat.com/2025/10/15/massive-fire-guts-sonalben-khakrawala-factory-in-santej-gidc — 15 Oct 2025 — (snippet) Santej GIDC, short circuit suspected.
  - Social engagement (Reddit/YouTube): NOT REACHABLE this session.
- Hard number: 5,888 fire deaths in 2024, 17.2% from short circuits, 149 factory fire incidents (Dataful/NCRB, 5 Jun 2026, fetched). The factory-fire death count is not in the page; factory share of deaths is NOT FOUND.
- Who has tried: NOT SEARCHED.
- Deep-tech wedge (one line): a thermal-IR + current-signature node for distribution boards and machine panels (MLX90640-class thermal array plus arc/leakage current sensing) that detects a hot busbar or loose lug and sends a WhatsApp or SMS alarm, built for owners who will not buy an audit.
- Overnight feasibility (1-5): 5. Thermal array + ESP32 + threshold/trend alarm + a heated-resistor demo is straightforward; real arc-fault detection is harder.
- Rough market pointer: Gujarat about 31,500 factories (party-quoted figure, Gujarat Samachar 17 Jun 2026). Illustrative only, my estimate: Rs 5-10k per panel, several panels per unit. No national panel count found.
- Risks / regulation: Fire services acts and NOC regime; false alarms; the sensor does not fix the root cause (illegal wiring, no licence).
