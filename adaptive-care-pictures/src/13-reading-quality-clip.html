A bad reading must never change a dose
A small clip on the cuff checks how the reading was taken. The clip is an ESP32 board with a motion sensor, built from the maker kit.
Three checks on every reading
1
Arm at heart level
The clip reads the angle of the upper arm. The arm must rest on a table, not hang or float.
Angle within range
2
Still for 5 minutes before the reading
A rest timer runs before the cuff inflates. Movement starts the timer again.
Rest timer 5:00
3
No movement or talking during inflation
Movement while the cuff inflates marks the reading. The phone asks the patient to sit quietly.
Motion stays low
Reading counts
All three checks pass. The reading goes to the engine.
Repeat the reading
Any check fails. The reading is kept but never moves a dose.
