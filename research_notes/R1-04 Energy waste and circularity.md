## Problem 1: Smart / prepaid meter bill shock with no way for a household to verify the meter
- Track: Frontier Hardware & Climate (also AI & Systems)
- Who suffers: Households and small shops in UP, Maharashtra, MP, Himachal, Assam, Bihar, Rajasthan, Gujarat. UP alone has installed over 8.45 million smart meters, about 7.55 million prepaid (target 30 million); nationwide 52.8 million smart meters, 39 million under RDSS as of 31 Dec 2025 (ThePrint, 9 Apr 2026). Symptoms: bills 2x to 100x usual after installation, auto-disconnection on small negative balance, supply not restored for 12 hours to 7 days after recharge.
- How they cope today: Complain to discom (ignored), ask for a check meter, escalate to CGRF/Ombudsman or consumer commission, protest (throwing meters out, a groom's wedding procession to the DM office), pay under protest. No independent consumer-side measurement; discoms answer with in-house tests (MSEDCL "Accu-Check" declared meters "technically flawless").

- Risks / regulation: Evidence admissibility and CEA metering regulations; UP's prepaid rollback (May 2026) may reduce urgency there, but Maharashtra, MP, Himachal, Assam rollouts continue per the dated reports.
