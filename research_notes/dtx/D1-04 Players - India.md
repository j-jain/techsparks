## Digest
1. No player found in India that changes a drug dose from home readings as a running, evidenced service. The one product built for it is Sanofi's My Dose Coach (basal insulin, clinician sets the plan, app proposes the dose from fasting glucose). Indian real-world data exist only as a 2018 conference abstract. CDSCO status NOT FOUND. (D1-04-F09, depth 4, SNIPPET)
2. The only CDSCO-licensed digital therapeutics found are Lupin's Lyfe (Class C, cardiac rehab, announced 25 Apr 2024) and Huma's configurable platform (Class C, 27 Aug 2024, disease-agnostic). None for diabetes or hypertension was found. (D1-04-F15, F30, OPENED)
3. Diabetes programmes (Sugar.fit, BeatO, Fitterfly, Twin Health) sell CGM or glucometer plus doctor and coach. None of their pages says CGM or glucose data changes the drug dose by an algorithm. Twin Health says users reduce or stop medicines (90%+, THEIR CLAIM, footnote missing). Depth 3 on what is published. (D1-04-F02, F04, F06, F07)
4. Home hypertension: the government's IHCI/Simple app runs a fixed drug-and-dose protocol with a registry and reminders. 24,31,962 hypertension patients registered by Oct 2022; 47% controlled (2019-2022 analysis). No private home-BP titration service found. (D1-04-F19, SNIPPET)
5. Shut: PhableCare (the best-known BP and chronic-care app lead) stopped work Feb 2023 for lack of funds, workforce 2,000 to ~200 after raising $25M in Apr 2022. Wellthy was bought by TruDoc (Feb 2024). (D1-04-F14, F23, F21)
6. Generic semaglutide launched 21-22 Mar 2026 from ₹1,290 a month. Companion offers: Healthify (tirzepatide, ₹48,000 for 3 months, doctors set dose), BeatO GLP-1 programme, Tracky (CGM and wearables to help clinicians titrate the dose, 23 Mar 2026). Dr Reddy's offers titration guidance through centres, no app. (D1-04-F10, F11, F06, F26, F27)
7. Surprises: (a) Phable gone, so the "home BP leader" lead is dead; (b) Tracky is the first Indian product found that names dose titration as its purpose for GLP-1 patients; (c) Lupin's Adhero smart inhaler dates from 2019 and shows data plus AQI alerts, not therapy changes.
Contradicts leads: Phable is shut (not alive); Wellthy was acquired, not merged; "Svaas" NOT FOUND; "a:care" NOT FOUND; Lupin Lyfe is cardiac, not diabetes.
