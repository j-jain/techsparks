StepICU

District ICUs that run safely with the staff they have.

Jaidityaa Jain · Krishang Goel

StepICU is a working name. One line: we make India’s new district ICUs run safely with the staff they actually have. Written protocols run step by step, a remote specialist approves only what needs a specialist, and the patient is followed from the ward, through the ICU, to home. It combines Cloudphysician-style remote critical care with Cadence-style protocol loops. Source: SPARK Night 2026 – smart ICU network, idea check (16 Nov 2026).
