# D1-05 Players - abroad and failures (agent: Sonnet 5.5, 2026-11-13)

## Digest
Facts that matter most for building tonight (IDs refer to Findings):
1. PHTI hypertension (Oct 2024): only programmes with a virtual team that adjusts medicine (Cadence, Ochsner, Story Health) gave clinically meaningful SBP falls (5 mmHg or more is the bar), certainty higher; monitoring-only and coaching-only did not. D1-05-F1, F2.
2. PHTI diabetes (Mar 2024): monitoring and coaching programmes cut HbA1c only 0.23 to 0.60 points and raised net spending; Virta (nutrition plus medicine reduction) was the only positive, with lower certainty. D1-05-F4, F5.
3. PHTI MSK (Jun 2024): physical-therapist-guided programmes effective and possibly cost-saving; remote-monitoring add-on PT costs more. D1-05-F9.
4. PHTI CKD (Sep 2026): no consistent benefit; about 1 in 1,000 gains a planned dialysis start. D1-05-F10.
5. Money is real at scale: Hinge Q3 2026 revenue $234.97M, profitable; Omada $91.3M, 1,134,000 members, Cigna affiliates $53.7M of the quarter; both paid mainly by employers and plans per member or engagement fee. D1-05-F12, F13, F15, F16.
6. Prescription-app failures: Pear (about $1,195 per course, payers refused, Medicare no cover, assets sold for about $6M); Akili cut 40% of staff; panel verdict: tried to change care and payment at once. D1-05-F20, F21, F22.
7. Livongo write-down: Teladoc booked $13.4B goodwill impairment in 2022 after paying $18.5B in 2020. D1-05-F53, F7.
8. Germany: 50 permanent plus 13 provisional DiGA (23 Sep 2026); about 1.9M prescribed 2020 to 2025; about EUR 400M spent; average negotiated price EUR 227; 18 of 81 removed for lack of benefit; from 1 Jan 2026 at least 20% of pay depends on performance. D1-05-F25 to F31.
9. Obesity apps dominate the German spend (Oviva EUR 101.9M, EUR 218.70 per course); NICE allows seven to eight apps that prescribe and monitor weight medicine, with evidence generation. D1-05-F28, F29, F39, F47.
10. Closed-loop insulin is the scaled device-plus-algorithm case: Insulet Q3 2026 $842M, Tandem $283.7M, Beta Bionics $35.5M. D1-05-F41 to F43.
11. Japan: CureApp HT reimbursed since 1 Sep 2022 (price conflicts: 7,010 yen vs about 2,490 per month); Susmed insomnia app cover from 1 Jun 2026. D1-05-F33, F35.
12. Virta: $160M+ annualised revenue, 550 organisations, guarantees on GLP-1 spend; clinician-led deprescribing is the model (THEIR CLAIM). D1-05-F18.

Surprises: (a) DarioHealth revenue is only $5.3M a quarter and shrinking in services despite earlier $238M funding (F45, F7). (b) Omada's filing says third-party providers prescribe obesity medicines, not Omada staff (F14). (c) Some DiGA listing counts differ by 24 depending on definition (F26).
Contradicts leads: lead 1 holds for hypertension, only partly for diabetes (F66). Hinge and Omada did list (leads confirmed). Pear confirmed. Onduo shut (F57) and Livongo-brand wind-down not confirmed.
