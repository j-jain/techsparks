### 2.2 The five on the poster

#### Vibhore Sharma — Capital 2B and Redstart Labs

- **Who he is.** An early member of the Naukri.com team, later CTO of Info Edge, briefly at AWS, then a deep-tech investor ([Entrepreneur India speaker page](https://www.entrepreneurindia.com/2023/vibhore.php)). The poster says "Redstart Labs" and the site says "Partner, Capital 2B". Both are true: the India Deep Tech Alliance lists him as someone who "runs Redstart Labs and serves as a Fund Manager at Capital 2B" ([idtalliance.org](https://idtalliance.org/)).
- **The two vehicles.**
  - Capital 2B is a $75 million deep-tech fund (same speaker page). Its backing by Info Edge and Temasek and its pre-seed to Series A range come from a 2022 YourStory piece we could not open (snippet).
  - Redstart Labs is Info Edge's own deep-tech investing arm. Info Edge approved Rs 100 crore more for it on 3 November 2025 ([Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/info-edge-to-infuse-inr-100-cr-in-investment-arm-redstart-labs/)).
- **What they have backed.**
  - Redstart: Unbox Robotics (warehouse robots), CynLr (robot vision), Skylark Drones, ePlane (electric air taxis), BrainSight AI (brain MRI) (same Inc42 page).
  - Capital 2B: Manastu Space, Botsync (warehouse robots), Ahammune (drug discovery), Beatoven.ai, HyperNorm AI ([Inc42 portfolio page](https://inc42.com/company/capital-2b/investments/)). It also led Leumas's seed in June 2025 and took part in the voice-AI company Ringg's round in August 2026, which Peak XV led ([Ringg's post](https://www.ringg.ai/blog/ringg-ai-extended-series-a-announcement)).
  - Info Edge as a whole held 30 deep-tech companies and Rs 455 crore invested at 31 March 2026 ([Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/info-edges-ai-deeptech-playbook-%e2%82%b91003-cr-portfolio-across-54-startups/)).
- **What he has said.** One verified line, on why Capital 2B led Leumas's $2.2 million seed: "They are solving a global challenge: manufacturing that is slow, expensive, and hard to scale" ([Entrepreneur India, 11 Jun 2025](https://india.entrepreneur.com/news-and-trends/leumas-raises-22-million-to-build-software-driven-robot/493075)). In 2022 he told YourStory that deep-tech startups have yes-or-no goals and an investor has to understand whether the technology will work (paraphrase; the page would not open).
- **Inference.**
  - He will care more about a measured result than a market slide. The event asks for "a tested result" by noon; give him the number, the sample size and where it fails.
  - Both portfolios pair hardware or sensing with ML. A camera, microphone or sensor feeding a model fits his history better than a chat interface.
  - Manufacturing is the one subject he has spoken on. Ideas M3 and F4 below sit there.
- **A question to expect.** "What exactly did you measure, on how many samples, and what breaks it?"

#### Karthik Varada — Beyond Next Ventures

- **Who he is.** Senior Associate in the India investment group of Beyond Next Ventures, a Japanese deep-tech fund ([team page](https://beyondnextventures.com/team/karthik-varada/)). He studied engineering at IIT Hyderabad and took a master's and a PhD at the University of Tokyo on impact and ESG investing. The India team has a second Karthik (Rampalli) with the same title, so check the surname before quoting anything.
- **The fund.** It backs founders "solving social issues with deep-science and deep-tech", from pre-seed to Series A, and links Indian startups to Japanese companies ([India page](https://beyondnextventures.com/india/)). Its first India-only fund has made two deals: Volt14, silicon-anode battery cells ([14 May 2025](https://beyondnextventures.com/news/beyond-next-ventures-volt14)), and FinalLayer, AI video tools ([27 Oct 2025](https://india.entrepreneur.com/news-and-trends/beyond-next-ventures-invests-in-ai-startup-finallayer/498777)). The older India portfolio includes Biomoneta (a device that kills airborne pathogens), Myelin Foundry (AI on edge devices) and Faunatech (mastitis detection in dairy cattle).
- **What he has said.** Only one piece, from December 2022: impact investing can return both social and financial gains, private money must help because public services cannot keep up, and he wants to connect Japanese research with Indian talent ([his post](https://beyondnextventures.com/insight/karthik-varada), paraphrased). Nothing from 2024–26 was found.
- **Inference.**
  - State the outcome in health, climate or resource terms: litres saved, cases found, kilowatt-hours.
  - Show the path out of the lab: who validates it, who certifies it, who buys it.
  - A Japan angle (a Japanese company that would use it) will register with this fund in particular.
- **A question to expect.** "What is the measurable impact, and what stands between this prototype and a product someone can buy?"

#### Shilpy Kochhar — Ashoka Research & Development Office

- **Who she is.** Director of Ashoka's Research & Development Office. She studied biochemistry and biotechnology (IIT Roorkee) and spent over a decade at BIRAC, the government's biotech funding body, most recently leading its partnerships and communications ([Ashoka profile](https://www.ashoka.edu.in/profile/shilpy-kochhar/); [BIRAC page](https://www.birac.nic.in/about_partnership.php)). A 2019 interview says she ran BIRAC's Ignition Grant, regional centres and student entrepreneurship programmes (snippet; the page would not open).
- **The grant she knows best.** BIRAC's Biotechnology Ignition Grant gives up to Rs 50 lakh over up to 18 months, with calls announced on 1 January and 1 July ([birac.nic.in/big.php](https://www.birac.nic.in/big.php)). No notice for a January 2027 call was out on 12 November.
- **What she has said.** No public quote found.
- **Inference.**
  - A Bio & Health prototype with a real assay, device or diagnostic is the natural fit.
  - Her office's job is finding grants. A one-page thesis that names the scheme, the stage and the ask will read well.
  - Expect questions on validation: against what gold standard, on how many samples, under which regulator.
- **A question to expect.** "How would you validate this, and which grant pays for that step?"

#### Anuj Mehta — Campus Fund

- **Who he is.** Campus Fund's own team page lists him under "Investments" and says he earlier led India investments at Rise Capital ([team page](https://yourcampusfund.com/team/)). Other sites call him Vice President or Investment Director; the firm's page uses neither.
- **The fund.** It backs students, recent graduates and dropouts. Fund III targets $100 million with first cheques of Rs 1–8 crore ([CXO Digital Pulse, 23 Jun 2025](https://www.cxodigitalpulse.com/campus-fund-launches-100m-fund-to-back-student-and-dropout-founders-across-india/)). Up to $10 million of it is set aside for first cheques to founders building from India ([Entrepreneur India, 23 Sep 2026](https://india.entrepreneur.com/business-news/campus-fund-earmarks-usd-10-mn-for-founders-who-build-in-india)). Its portfolio includes Digantara (space tracking), EtherealX (reusable rockets), Sarla Aviation (air taxis) and Serendipity Space (drug processing in microgravity). Its 2025 report says deep tech was nearly 17% of the 7,300-plus startups it evaluated (snippet).
- **What he has said.** Nothing found. The fund's quotes come from its founder, Richa Bajpai.
- **Inference.**
  - A team of current students is exactly whom this fund exists for.
  - It has backed rockets and aircraft from student founders, so a hardware build is no handicap.
  - He is likely to test whether the team will keep going after the night.
- **A question to expect.** "Will you still be building this in three months, and what would the first cheque buy?"

#### "Aman Makhani" — PedalStart

- **Who he is.** Unconfirmed. PedalStart's site names no one called Aman, and no page ties the name on the poster to a role there. One search surfaced LinkedIn and directory listings for an "Aman Mankani" at PedalStart in Gurugram (snippet), which a second check could not reproduce. Ask the organisers.
- **The firm.** PedalStart is a Gurugram accelerator and founder community started by Manas Pal and Aditya Darolia. It is not a deep-tech programme.
- **The firm's view.** In January 2026 Pal wrote: "Raising money did not fix broken products, services, weak sales, or confused teams" ([SMEStreet, 9 Jan 2026](https://smestreet.in/infocus/from-capital-to-capability-how-accelerators-redefined-value-creation-in-2025-10984602)). The same piece lists the usual founder mistakes as hiring too early, too many features, the wrong customers and wrong pricing, and says investors now ask about cash, monthly revenue and retention.
- **Inference (about the firm, not the person).** Have a named first customer, what they pay today for the workaround, and what you would charge.
- **A question to expect.** "Who pays for this first, and what do they pay now?"

### 2.3 The four listed only on the site

- **Arshia Mal, WTFund.** An Ashoka alumna who founded Soulswap ([Ashoka profile](https://www.ashoka.edu.in/profile/arshia-mal/)). WTFund gives Rs 20 lakh with no equity to founders under 25. Its cohort profiled in January 2026 leaned to health hardware and AI: an oral-cancer screening test, an AI device for pathology slides, a stroke-rehab robot, a desktop pick-and-place machine, and CCTV incident alerts ([Odisha Plus, 31 Jan 2026](https://odisha.plus/2026/01/nikhil-kamath-wtfund-young-founders-deeptech-healthcare-india/)). **Inference:** a working physical or health prototype from an under-25 team is what this fund already picks.
- **Aishwarya Malhi, Rebalance.** She co-founded this accelerator and angel network for women founders in 2019. A May 2026 interview gives 10 cohorts, more than 30 startups, about 40% of them deep tech, and the line "Good investing is not gendered. Access is." ([Indian Startup Times, 23 May 2026](https://www.indianstartuptimes.com/?p=19811)). **Inference:** teams with women founders and women's-health problems are her stated priority.
- **Vidushi Malhotra and Apoorv Bamba, Belong Education.** Co-founders of an employability platform focused on skill-first hiring in Tier 2–3 India ([ANI release, 12 Oct 2022](https://theprint.in/ani-press-releases/belong-education-acquires-internin-to-double-up-its-mission-of-bringing-skill-first-hiring-to-tier-2-3-india/1165049)). **Inference:** they are likely to press on who the user is and how you reach them, more than on technical depth.

### 2.4 What the room as a whole rewards

Read together, the nine records point to five things. Each is cheap to prepare.

1. **A number you measured that night.** The event itself asks for a tested result, and the one investor with a stated filter asks whether the technology works.
2. **A sensor or a device feeding a model.** Almost every deep-tech company these funds hold pairs hardware with ML.
3. **A named payer.** Say who buys it, what they pay today, and what you would charge.
4. **An outcome in plain units.** Cases found, litres saved, rupees recovered.
5. **A next step with a named funder.** The table below lists the ones in the room.
