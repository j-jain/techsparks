# D1-06 Buildable by noon: signals, open data, simulators, code and protocols for four adaptive loops (agent: claude-opus-5-5, 2026-11-13)

## Digest
Twelve facts:
1. Gujarat HEARTS protocol (India) gives an exact 5-step ladder with 30-day re-checks, threshold bands and safety branches (D1-06-F14).
2. IHCI Punjab and Maharashtra ladders differ at step 2. Their control rates by step (70% to 77%; 76% to 82%) come from 159,292 patients and are CC BY (F01-F04).
3. Simple's open code (MIT, alive Nov 2026) stores a protocol as a drug list plus `follow_up_days`. It has no step logic (F40).
4. Omron HEM-7156T costs about ₹2,500 to ₹2,900 (F31). It is readable from a laptop with omblepy, through a custom protocol rather than the standard BP service. Two Connect models are encrypted (F07).
5. ADA Fig 9.4 basal rule: start 10 units or 0.1-0.2 U/kg, then "increase 2 units every 3 days". If hypoglycaemia has no clear cause, cut 10-20% (F16). The ICMR STW gives targets only, with no titration schedule (F17).
6. ShanghaiT2DM has 100 T2D patients with CGM and insulin records. It is open on figshare and needs no application (F11).
7. simglucose is MIT, type 1 only, with 30 patients and a Gym interface (F10). No open T2D basal simulator was found (F30).
8. AAMOS-00 is CC BY. It has smart peak-flow, inhaler and watch data, with weather and air quality joined: 22 patients, 2,054 days (F50).
9. GINA 2025 gives inhaler-increase rules and care triggers word for word. It has no printed 80/60 zones (F20-F23).
10. A DIY ESP32 spirometer (n = 30) measures FEV1 well (MAPE 5%) but PEF poorly (MAPE 18%, -52 L/min) (F24).
11. Cardiac rehab rule without a stress test: RHR+20-30 bpm, RPE 11-14, and raise intensity if RPE <11 (F36).
12. HeartSteps (CC BY) plus MABWiser (Apache) allow an offline replay of adaptive nudging (F44, F55).

Three surprises:
- Omron does not use the standard BP profile.
- Simple has no step engine.
- The UI-PRMD rehab dataset page now returns 404 (F27).

Against starting lead 3:
- Loop (b) has no type 2 simulator.
- Loop (c) has the best open longitudinal data.
- A home peak-flow device built tonight would be weakest on exactly the metric the plan uses.

### Q5a. Hypertension protocol (loop a): IHCI state protocols
- D1-06-F01. IHCI Punjab protocol, steps quoted from the paper: start amlodipine 5 mg; if uncontrolled, amlodipine 10 mg; then add telmisartan 40 mg (to 80 mg); then add chlorthalidone 12.5 mg (to 25 mg). Quote: "all individuals diagnosed with hypertension were started on amlodipine 5 mg." Year 2022 data. Kaur et al., Global Heart 19(1):30, 19 Mar 2024, CC BY 4.0. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10959138/ OPENED. CITED.
- D1-06-F02. IHCI Maharashtra protocol: amlodipine 5 mg; add telmisartan 40 mg; telmisartan 80 mg; amlodipine 10 mg; add chlorthalidone 6.25 mg then 12.5 mg. Same source, OPENED. CITED.
- D1-06-F03. Step interval and target: patients "reviewed after 30 days" (Punjab) / "reviewed every 30 days" (Maharashtra); control = "SBP was <140 mmHg and DBP was <90 mmHg". Same source, OPENED. CITED.
- D1-06-F04. Control by step (cumulative, follow-up Jan-Mar 2022): Punjab 70% at amlodipine 5 mg, 75% at 10 mg, 77% after telmisartan 40 mg, plateau 77% from step 4; Maharashtra 76% at amlodipine 5 mg, 81% after telmisartan 40 mg, plateau 82% from step 3. n = 159,292 analysed (Punjab 33,450; Maharashtra 125,842) of 281,209 started. Same source, OPENED. CITED. These per-step percentages are a ready-made reference curve for a simulated cohort.
- D1-06-F05. Protocols across IHCI states share amlodipine 5 mg start (telmisartan first for CKD) and diverge at step 2 (amlodipine 10 mg vs add telmisartan 40 mg); one state chose enalapril. Search summary of medRxiv preprint https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.1101/2023.08.17.23294195.full.pdf, SNIPPET.

- D1-06-F14. Gujarat hypertension protocol (WHO HEARTS, India), text extracted from the PDF: "High BP: SBP 140 or DBP 90 mmHg". "Check for compliance at each visit before titration of dose or addition of drugs". Step 1 "Prescribe Amlodipine 5mg"; Step 2 "After 30 days measure BP again. If still high: Increase to Amlodipine 10mg"; Step 3 "Add Telmisartan 40mg"; Step 4 "Increase to Telmisartan 80mg**"; Step 5 "Add Chlorthalidone 12.5mg***"; then "Check if the patient has been taking medications regularly and correctly. If yes, refer to a specialist." Footnotes: "If SBP 180 or DBP 110, refer patient to a specialist after starting treatment"; "If SBP 160-179 or DBP 100-109, start treatment on the same day"; "If SBP 140-159 or DBP 90-99, check on a different day and if still elevated, start treatment"; ** blood sugar, urea, creatinine, urine protein before raising telmisartan; *** "Hydrochlorothiazide 25 mg can be used if Chlorthalidone 12.5 mg is not available". Pregnancy: "DO NOT give Telmisartan or Chlorthalidone." "Dispense drugs for 30 days and give appointment after 4 weeks". Resolve to Save Lives fact sheet 759_CVH_0225_Rev A (Feb 2025), https://resolvetosavelives.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/759_CVH_HEARTS-in-India_Fact-Sheet_0225_Rev-A_v10.pdf OPENED (PDF text extracted locally). CITED. Note: the symbols for ">=" were lost in extraction; read "SBP 140" as SBP >=140.
- D1-06-F15. HEARTS-model Nigeria protocol (RTSL; PDF metadata title says Bangladesh, created 4 Dec 2019) for a losartan variant: Step 1 "Start amlodipine 5 mg."; Step 2 "After 1 month, measure BP again. If still high, Treat with amlodipine 5 mg + losartan 50 mg."; Step 3 amlodipine 10 mg + losartan 100 mg; Step 4 + HCTZ 25 mg; Step 5 "Refer for specialist hypertension management."
