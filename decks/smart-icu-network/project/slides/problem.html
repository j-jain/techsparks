Say: The government is building Critical Care Blocks in almost every district. It is not building the specialists to run them. India has about one ICU specialist for every six it needs, and from this year the states, not the centre, pay the staff. So beds sit idle, or patients die waiting to be moved. Sources: - 621 Critical Care Blocks approved, Rs 18,799.21 crore, FY 2021-22 to 2025-26. Lok Sabha reply, Dec 2025 (search summary only; a second summary agrees). D6-01-F03. - The 2022 block guideline plans blocks for 602 districts and lists a monitor with a central station for every ICU bed. D6-01-F01. - 8,350 critical care specialists against a need of more than 50,000 (IMA via Reuters, 2016 figures). D6-01-F09. - 1 in 6 = 8,350 / 50,000 = 16.7%. 41,650+ = 50,000 minus 8,350. OUR ARITHMETIC. - Central money for staff ends in FY 2025-26, that is March 2026. D6-01-F02 (guideline text, confirmed in the C4 claims check). - Idle machines: about 40% of Covid-era ventilators unused or scrapped in Madhya Pradesh medical colleges (2025). D6-01-F11.
