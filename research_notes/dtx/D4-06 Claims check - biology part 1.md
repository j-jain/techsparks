# D4-06 Claims check - biology part 1 (agent: claude-opus-5-5, 2026-11-14)

## Summary

Checked 96 fact IDs: every fact named in the Digest and "What this suggests for the product" sections of D4-01, D4-03 and D4-05, plus D3-04-F09 for the GRAND clash. Each source was re-opened by a second route. Abstracts came from PubMed E-utilities efetch. Full texts came from Europe PMC fullTextXML, NCBI efetch db=pmc or the PMC article page. Labels came from the DailyMed SPL XML service (v2), and the guidelines from the KDIGO 2024 and ICMR NEDL PDFs, the NICE NG136 page and openFDA 510(k).

Counts: CONFIRMED 93, CORRECTED 2, CONTESTED 0, NOT FOUND 1.

The research notes recorded their sources faithfully. Every number in the six named checks matched the source. All OUR ARITHMETIC sums in D4-03 (F34, F57, F61, F62) and D4-01 (F49, F50, F21) hold when redone.

Corrections:
1. GRAND (D3-04-F09 CORRECTED; D4-03-F40 CONFIRMED). Both counts are right but measure different things. The study **enrolled 1,920** treated patients at 18 centres in 12 states (7 regions). Only **1,578 with valid clinic and home readings were analysed**, and every percentage is of the 1,578: 55.9% well controlled, 5.1% white-coat uncontrolled, 21.8% masked uncontrolled, 17.2% sustained uncontrolled, 77.7% vs 55.9% controlled. D3-04-F09 attaches these percentages to "1,920 treated patients", which is wrong. Published online 6 Nov 2026 (Hypertens Res). The 1,920 figure is the denominator of the companion knowledge paper (D4-03-F41).
2. D4-05-F11 (OUTREACH). 748 people (not 720) were excluded after visit 1, and 720 of them because they were adherent or their status was unconfirmed. This does not change a product rule.

Cautions that bear on product rules (the facts are recorded correctly, but these points limit their use):
- The "<10 mmHg fall after a drug is added" trigger (D4-05-F14) is Lane et al.'s rule for **ordering a confirmatory drug assay** in patients on 2 drugs. It is not a non-adherence classifier. At protocol step 3 (adding telmisartan 40 to amlodipine 10), the mean observed fall in the label trial was 11.1 mmHg, of which 3.7 beyond staying on amlodipine 10 (D4-01-F50). So many adherent patients will fall less than 10 mmHg, and the rule will flag them often.
- The "77% to 85% per added drug" figure (D4-05-F03) comes from an abstract that says "increase in nonadherence" without naming odds or risk; the full text could not be opened. It is per prescribed drug, not per dose step (steps 2 and 4 add no drug). The product line "risk rises about 1.8 times" turns odds into risk, and the Brazil figure is 1.57 (F07).
- The KDIGO 2024 recheck rule (D4-05-F27, F28) is quoted exactly. It is a **CKD** guideline practice point and says "depending on the current GFR and serum potassium". A >30% creatinine rise (PP 3.6.4) is not the same size as a >30% eGFR fall (PP 2.1.4).
- The noise bands in D4-03-F33/F34 come from **untreated** IDH participants (mean home BP 115/77). Treated older patients vary more (F10), so the 8.5 and 12.5 mmHg change thresholds are probably too narrow for the loop's patients.
- D4-03-F11/F61: 4.7 and 5.1 mmHg are the mixed model's **residual SD around each person's own fitted monthly trend**. The paper calls this "short-term intra-participant BP variability" and describes it as the SD of "any 1-week set" (HOMEBP) or of a calendar-month set (TASMINH4). It never says "SD of monthly means". The F61 sums hold, but its stated reason ("includes slow real drift") does not: each person's linear drift is already in the model.
- The seasonal figures are not home-cuff data. Punjab (D4-03-F31): trained nurses with a sphygmomanometer at home or the workplace, 9 am to 2 pm, mean of 2 readings 5 min apart, in a general community sample (hypertension prevalence 10% to 24%), not treated patients. Delhi (F32): readings at 4 investigator home visits in normotensive women aged 18 to 40.
- D4-01-F03: the "generally much larger" variation sentence is in PHYSIC's Introduction and cites earlier studies; it is not a PHYSIC result. PHYSIC's 4.4 mmHg is model-based ("assuming the fitted model to be true").
