## 7. Method and limits

### 7.1 How this was done

- **Round 1, five research agents.** Two on the nine mentors and their institutions, one on Indian proptech, two on startups funded since January 2025 by Y Combinator, a16z, Sequoia Capital and Peak XV. 163 web searches.
- **Triage.** Fourteen ideas were shortlisted: four read from the mentors' records, five proptech, five Indian versions of funded startups.
- **Round 2, four agents.** Two re-opened the sources behind the shortlist and tried to break each claim. Two designed each idea in depth: the system, the model and data, the Indian differences, the business arithmetic and an overnight test. 130 web searches.
- **Writing.** The report was written from the notes after applying every correction from round 2.

### 7.2 What round 2 changed

| Round 1 said | Round 2 found |
|---|---|
| No Indian AI tool audits health claims | Consint.AI, Vitraya, Superclaims and insurers' own systems do |
| No Indian architect-side plan checker | Haryana's portal gives it free; SoftTech and others sell it. Idea dropped |
| Solo clinics are an open gap for a scribe | Eka Care's scribe prints prescriptions in Hindi, with a free tier. Idea reduced |
| A title reader in regional languages is open | Landeed sells it to banks; Telugu OCR is about 88% at best. Idea reduced |
| Rs 1.26 lakh crore of rental deposits | A modelled estimate with no published method |
| MahaRERA ordered carpet-area refunds | It was an advocate's opinion column |
| SWAMIH 2.0 is Rs 650 crore | The fund is Rs 15,000 crore; the smaller figure appears in one headline |
| Non-revenue water is 40–50% | The best source gives a 38% urban average |
| Sequoia led Form Energy's round | It put $100 million into a $750 million round led by T. Rowe Price |
| a16z led XBuild; Sequoia backed Structured AI | a16z only took part; Sequoia's scout programme only took part |
| Puralink is unfunded | It is a Sydney company with a $2.3 million seed |
| Anuj Mehta is Vice President or Investment Director | The fund's own page says only "Investments" |

### 7.3 What could not be checked

- **The mentors' own words.** Only Vibhore Sharma has a verified quote from 2024–26. Section 2's reading of the others rests on what their funds hold and on their careers.
- **The PedalStart mentor.** No page confirms the name on the poster or his role.
- **SPARK Night itself.** No challenge statements, judges, criteria or details of the Singapore programme are public. Ask the organisers on the day.
- **Pages that would not open.** YourStory, LinkedIn, Instagram, X, Reddit and several others blocked the tools. Facts from them are marked "(snippet)": they were seen only in a search summary.
- **Market sizes.** Almost every market figure in the idea cards uses at least one assumed count (shops, hospitals, cities). They are marked "(assumption)" and are there so you can change the inputs, not as findings.
- **Model accuracy in India.** For most ideas no published Indian accuracy figure exists. That is why each card names one number to measure overnight.
- **Things recalled, not looked up.** Dataset sizes for the bare-board PCB sets, the list of phones with a depth sensor, and the wording of two RERA sections came from the design agents' memory and are flagged in their notes.
