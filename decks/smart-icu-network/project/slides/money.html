Say: The state already budgets about 60 lakh rupees a year for five specialists in each 50-bed block, and it struggles to fill those posts. We charge 15,000 rupees per monitored bed a month, which is 28.8 lakh a year for a block: about half. Of each 15,000, about 7,000 pays for the remote specialist, about 1,900 covers the kit, cloud and field support, and about 6,100 is margin. That margin depends on one thing: how many beds one specialist covers. Because specialists only see exceptions, we target 150 beds per specialist seat. At 80, which is what remote ICUs manage today, we would only break even. Top half (sources): Rs 60 lakh a year for 5 specialists per 50-bed block (block guideline, Annexure 3; D6-01-F02). Rs 15,000 per bed a month is an ASSUMPTION. 16 x Rs 15,000 x 12 = Rs 28.8 lakh (OUR ARITHMETIC); 28.8 / 60 = 48%. Bars to scale. Cost per monitored bed per month (all OUR ESTIMATE, to test in the pilot): - Remote specialist, Rs 7,000: intensivist cost about Rs 2.5 lakh a month (salary sites report about Rs 24 to 36 lakh a year for 6 to 9 years’ experience; self-reported, search summary only). A 24/7 seat needs about 4.2 people (168 hours a week / 40). Rs 2.5 lakh x 4.2 = Rs 10.5 lakh a month per seat, / 150 beds = Rs 7,000. - Kit, Rs 600: 2MP IP camera about Rs 3,150 (marketplace listing, search summary only) + half a tablet (Samsung Galaxy Tab A9 launched at Rs 12,999; one per two beds) Rs 6,500 + mounts and cabling about Rs 2,000 + local server and UPS about Rs 1.6 lakh per block / 16 beds = Rs 10,000. Total about Rs 21,650 a bed, spread over 36 months = about Rs 600. - Cloud and connectivity, Rs 500: block internet about Rs 3,000 a month / 16 beds = Rs 190, plus cloud, storage and SMS about Rs 310. - Support and training, Rs 800: one field engineer-trainer per 10 blocks (160 beds) at about Rs 50,000 a month = Rs 310, plus clinical protocol upkeep and quality checks about Rs 490. - Total cost about Rs 8,900. Margin Rs 6,100 = 41% of Rs 15,000. - Sensitivity: at 80 beds per seat the specialist line is Rs 13,125, total cost Rs 15,025: break-even. Cloudphysician reports 60 to 80 patients per intensivist (2020, search summary only; D6-02-F04). - Bar segments are drawn to scale (Rs 15,000 = full width). Why a hospital says yes: one PMJAY schedule pays about Rs 8,500 for an ICU day without a ventilator (search summary only; D6-03-F08), so one bed-month fee equals under two insured ICU days.
