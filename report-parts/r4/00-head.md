# SPARK Night 2026 — mentors, proptech and funded ideas

*Second research pack for the TechSparks Ashoka 2.0 overnight buildathon, 13–14 November 2026. Compiled 12 November 2026 from two rounds of research: nine agents and 293 web searches. It sits beside the 3 November report, "problems and ideas", and does not replace it. Working files are in `research_notes\`.*

## 1. Summary

### What the mentors want

Nobody has published it. SPARK Night's challenge statements, judges and criteria are not public, and of the five mentors on the poster, only one has a verified public quote since 2024. So section 2 reads each of the nine mentors (five on the poster, four more on the programme site) from what their fund has paid for. Five things come through.

1. **A number measured that night.** The event asks for a tested result, and Vibhore Sharma's stated filter is whether the technology works.
2. **A sensor or device feeding a model.** That describes nearly every deep-tech company these funds hold.
3. **A named payer,** and what they pay today.
4. **An outcome in plain units:** cases found, litres saved, rupees recovered.
5. **A next step with a named funder.** Three are tied to the mentors: WTFund (Rs 20 lakh, under-25s), BIRAC's Ignition Grant (up to Rs 50 lakh, biotech) and Campus Fund (Rs 1–8 crore, student founders).

### Four things worth knowing before you choose

- **Six ideas hold up; none is a pure software company.** The six with a business score of 3 are mostly services that use AI to do paid work faster: inspect a flat, file a registration, prepare a claim, quote a job. Section 5 explains why Indian versions of funded startups keep ending up that way.
- **If you want AI/ML to carry the product,** the strongest are F2 (reading claims and quoting the policy), F4 (reading engineering drawings) and M1 (finding parasites on a slide). All three need a human to confirm the output, and each card explains why.
- **The best match between an idea and a mentor is one of yours.** Vibhore Sharma's only public quote is about manufacturing being "slow, expensive, and hard to scale". Your factory-floor voice idea, and F4 and M3 in this report, all sit there. Section 6 has the full mapping, including the eight finalists from 3 November.
- **"Nobody does this in India" was wrong more often than right.** The first round made that claim for seven ideas. A second search by name left it standing for one.

### A way to pick, by team

- AI/ML first, software only: **F2** or **F4**.
- Vision with a little hardware: **M3** or **P4**.
- A bio member leading: **M1**.
- Someone who knows maps: **P1**.
- The demo least likely to fail: **F1**'s pack-label checker, or **P4**'s area test.

### How to read this report

- **Inference** marks our reading of a mentor. It is not something they said.
- **(snippet)** marks a fact seen only in a search summary because the page would not open.
- **(assumption)** marks a number a design agent chose in order to do the arithmetic.
- Every idea card names **one number to measure overnight**. Where no published Indian accuracy figure exists, that measurement is the result.
