KEY facts (hypertension):
- KEY: Self-monitoring ALONE does not lower BP; with titration co-interventions it does. IPD meta-analysis, 25 trials IPD, 7,138 participants at 12 mo: overall -3.2 mmHg (-4.9 to -1.6); self-monitoring alone -1.0 mmHg (-3.3 to 1.2); with intensive support -6.1 mmHg (-9.0 to -3.2). Quote: "Self-monitoring alone is not associated with lower BP or better control". Tucker et al, PLoS Med 2017, PMID 28926573, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28926573/ (OPENED abstract, Sep 2017).
- KEY: Patient self-titration in high-risk patients: SBP -9.2 mmHg (5.7 to 12.7) at 12 mo vs usual care. TASMIN-SR, JAMA Aug 2014, PMID 25157723 (OPENED).
- KEY: Telemonitoring added nothing over self-monitoring when both feed GP titration: "No difference between the self-monitoring and telemonitoring groups" (-1.2 mmHg, -3.5 to 1.2). TASMINH4, Lancet Mar 2018, PMID 29499873 (OPENED). Implication: the value is in the titration step, not the data pipe.
- KEY: Non-physician titration at scale prevents events: CRHCP composite CVD HR 0.76 (0.72 to 0.81) over 7 years, 33,995 people, 326 villages. Circulation Aug 2026, PMID 42666029 (OPENED). Quote: "trained NPCHPs initiated and titrated antihypertensive medications using a standardized protocol under primary care physician supervision".
- KEY: Lay CHWs titrating with mobile decision support beat facility referral: control 58% vs 48%, aOR 1.52 (1.01 to 2.29). Lesotho, Nat Med Mar 2026, PMID 41680483 (OPENED).
- KEY (Japan): HERB-DH1, lifestyle-only DTx (CureApp), 24-h SBP -2.4 mmHg (-4.5 to -0.3) at 12 wk, n=390. Eur Heart J Oct 2021, PMID 34455443 (OPENED). First approved hypertension DTx in Japan per PMID 36207530 title "The first software as medical device of evidence-based hypertension digital therapeutics for clinical practice" (Hypertens Res Dec 2022; title only, OPENED listing).
- Village doctor mHealth for CV risk (not drug titration; goal-setting + monitoring with feedback): 10-yr ASCVD risk -1.88 points (-2.57 to -1.19); SBP -7.64 mmHg, n=4533, 127 villages. BMJ May 2025, PMID 40425261 (OPENED).
