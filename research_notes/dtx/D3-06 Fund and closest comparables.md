# D3-06 Fund and closest comparables (agent: Sonnet 5.5, 2026-11-13)

## Digest
Twelve facts that matter most:
1. BNV led Wellthy's June 2023 round (amount undisclosed); fund staff named: Yuichiro Sano and Karthik Varada (D3-06-F01). Answers the open question: yes.
2. BNV backed CureApp, Japan's prescription-app company, as its first fund's first investment in 2015 (D3-06-F26). CureApp HT is lifestyle coaching, not a dose change (D3-06-F19).
3. Closed Loop Medicine Ltd entered members' voluntary liquidation 28 Apr 2026; its website now points to Morchella Medical, a 2026 licensing company (D3-06-F07, F08).
4. Closed Loop's one trial: 205 adults, single arm, amlodipine titrated in an app, systolic -11 mmHg (95% CI 10 to 12), no control group (D3-06-F09).
5. Cadence: nurse-practitioner team did over 24,000 medication titrations under provider-approved pathways; 23,638 hypertension patients, control 20.5% to 36.6% in its full-medication tier (company claims) (D3-06-F12, F13).
6. Cadence HypertensionOS, protocol-bound titration software, entered FDA's TEMPO pilot 13 Aug 2026; explicitly not FDA cleared (D3-06-F31). Cadence raised US$100M Series C, Spark Capital lead, June 2026 (D3-06-F14, F34).
7. Omron (Japanese cuff maker) runs Hypertension Plus / Viso in the UK: generates a medication plan from home readings; clinician accepts or modifies (SNIPPET-grade, D3-06-F35).
8. Ochsner: 5,057 Medicare patients, control 57.1% to 76.1% (Black) and 61.3% to 77.7% (white), retrospective (D3-06-F17).
9. Story Health VITAL-HF: randomised, 178 HFrEF patients, no numbers on Duke page, funded by the owner (D3-06-F15).
10. Tricog GOALS-HF: 52 patients, observational, no results posted (D3-06-F32).
11. CureApp HT fee from 1 Jun 2026: 90 points face-to-face, 78 online, material price 7,010 yen (D3-06-F20).
12. Medicare ACCESS (from July 2026) pays technology-enabled chronic care; hypertension in the G0676 co-management track (D3-06-F36).

Surprises: the fund held both CureApp and Wellthy; the closest dose-titration UK company is in liquidation; the fund's pages show no India chronic-disease paper.
Contradicts leads: DT1 said Wellthy was only inferred through TruDoc; it is a direct lead investment.
