# D3-02 Claims check: D1-03 Burden and access gap, D1-04 Players - India, D2-05 Market and money (agent: Sonnet 5.5, 2026-11-13)

## Verdict
About 45 facts checked: 29 CONFIRMED, 7 CORRECTED, 5 CONTESTED, 3 NOT FOUND, plus 9 SNIPPET facts in D2-05 not upgraded (search cap reached).
Changes to the market model: (1) "315 million" is a 2021 projection (investigators' commentary; not in the paper's abstract). (2) "Under 1 in 5 treated" is 17.7% of all hypertensive adults, NFHS-5 2019-21, preprint. (3) 30.7% is a 2018 ceiling built on an ASSUMED 30% public share; a sister letter says at most 26%. (4) Rs 3,906 is the model price of one brand single pill, inside a Rs 2,500 to Rs 5,100 range. (5) 13,88,185 doctors is a December 2025 figure, not February 2026; the 80% is a ministry availability assumption for allopathic plus AYUSH.
Changes to the Decision brief: IHCI control is among returners only, no comparison; Tamil Nadu control denominators unstated; "specialist posts" means four specialities; semaglutide launched 20-21 Mar 2026.
Never say: "32.9% controlled" as part of one cascade; "1:778"; BeatO revenue; Twin Health 2026 round or US$1.4B; My Dose Coach 295/684; "only two Class C products in India".
