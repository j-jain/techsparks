Monitoring stops at the reading. A therapy loop changes the treatment.
The challenge asks for care that keeps adapting between appointments, "beyond monitoring and reminders". The difference is whether the reading comes back as a changed treatment.
Monitoring and reminders
1 Sense
A home reading
Pressure cuff, sugar meter, sensor.
→
2 Show
A chart and a nudge
"Your reading is high. Take your medicine."
→
3 Decide
Waits for the next visit
Weeks or months away, if the patient goes.
→
4 Act
Same treatment
The dose that was not working stays.
Therapy loop
1 Sense
A home reading
The same cheap device.
→
2 Decide
A protocol proposes the next step
Software applies a published treatment protocol to this patient's readings.
→
3 Confirm
A clinician approves
One tap by a doctor or trained health worker. Unsafe cases are sent up.
→
4 Act
The treatment changes
New dose, next medicine, new exercise level, in the patient's language.
5 Learn: the next readings show whether it worked, and the loop runs again
