# D3-01 Claims check: D1-01 and D1-02 clinical evidence (agent: Claude Sonnet 5.5, 2026-11-13)

## Verdict

Checked 53 fact IDs against the source (abstracts via NCBI E-utilities; PHTI PDFs; ResMed, AAFA, GOV.UK, SEC, ClinicalTrials.gov, trade pages). Status: CONFIRMED 48, CORRECTED 5, CONTESTED 0, NOT FOUND 0. Every number in the two notes matched its source. The corrections are precision fixes (a comparator, a date, a funder, a denominator), and five wordings in the Decision brief or DT1 need changing (listed under "Sentences to change"). Four SNIPPET facts were upgraded to OPENED: Propeller ceased operations, Teva Digihaler withdrawal, Rejoyn primary result, CRHCP incentives and free drugs.

Sentences to change in the Decision brief:
1. Tucker 6.1 mmHg: "with intensive co-interventions (titration by doctors, pharmacists or patients, plus education or counselling)", not "including dose changes".
2. STRONG-HF 15.2% vs 23.3%: adjusted, down-weighted Kaplan-Meier estimates at 180 days; high-intensity arm had four clinic visits and more adverse events (41% vs 29%).
3. "Telemonitoring without a therapy change" (TELE-HF, BEAT-HF): the abstracts do not show that. BEAT-HF had nurse "protocolized actions". Say "telemonitoring without a mandated dose protocol".
4. 3.3% at day 30: the median of 93 popular Android mental-health apps (2019), not "3.3% of users".
5. Rural China: add that the programme also gave discounted or free drugs and paid village doctors incentive supplements tied to control rates (now OPENED).

## Never say
- "Self-monitoring plus dose changes cut BP by 6.1 mmHg" (6.1 is any intensive co-intervention).
- "15.2% of patients against 23.3%" for STRONG-HF as if raw counts (raw: 74/506 and 109/502).
- "25% of Trivandrum heart-failure patients" (it is 25% of the 62% with reduced ejection fraction).
- "COBRA-BPS health workers changed doses" (they monitored and counselled; trained physicians treated).
- "Both heart-failure telemonitoring trials had no therapy change" (BEAT-HF protocolised actions).
- "Adherence rose from 30% to 84%" (two arms, not before and after).
- "3.3% of mental-health app users stay to day 30" (median app).
- "The China result is the protocol alone" (free or discounted drugs and incentives included).
- "Warfarin self-dosing beats self-testing" as a head-to-head result (separate trial sets, indirect).
- "Propeller shut down in <date>" (no date on the ResMed page); "ResMed closed Propeller purchase 31 Jan 2019" (press release is 7 Jan 2019).
- "PHTI says digital diabetes tools fail" without "non-continuous glucose monitor, type 2, US".
