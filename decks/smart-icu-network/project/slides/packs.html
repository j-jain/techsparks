Say: Nothing physical changes when we add a disease. The bed kit in the middle stays the same: camera, tablet, whatever monitor is there, and a specialist on call. A disease is a protocol file that plugs in. We launch with four. Sepsis is almost a third of ICU patients; its file runs the hour-1 bundle, then blood-pressure rechecks. Snakebite kills about 58,000 Indians a year; the government guideline is already a timer: a clotting test every hour for three hours, then every six. Poisoning has a trial where doubling the antidote every five minutes cut deaths from 22.5% to 8%. Heart attack follows Tamil Nadu’s network: ECG, clot-buster, then transfer, which cut one-year deaths by about a fifth. Each was picked by three tests: district ICUs see it, there is a written recipe, and a late step kills. Heart failure, warfarin and liver come next. How to read the slide: centre = the hardware every bed has. Each card = one protocol file: name, why it matters, its source, and a drawing of what the timer runs. Orange marks = the timed steps our system runs. Sources: - Sepsis: 29.3% of adults in the national INDICAPS-II snapshot (132 ICUs, 4,669 adults) had sepsis or septic shock during their stay; the sample leans to large hospitals. D6-01-F08. Recipe: Surviving Sepsis hour-1 bundle (search summary only). D6-03-F03. - Snakebite: about 58,000 deaths a year in India, 94% rural, 77% outside hospital (Million Death Study). D6-01-F14. Recipe: MoHFW Standard Treatment Guidelines 2016: clotting test “every 1 h from admission for three hours and then 6 hourly for 24 hours”. D6-03-F01. - Poisoning: atropine trial, Bangladesh, 156 patients: dose doubled every 5 minutes until signs of atropinisation, then an infusion. Deaths 8.0% vs 22.5%; time to stable 23.90 vs 151.74 minutes. One hospital, open-label. D6-03-F02. - Heart attack: Tamil Nadu STEMI hub-and-spoke programme, JAMA Cardiology 2017: 4 hubs, 35 spokes, 2,420 patients. One-year deaths 17.6% to 14.2%, a 19% relative drop (OUR ARITHMETIC). Before-and-after study; in-hospital deaths did not change. D6-02-F17. - Next packs: heart failure and warfarin reuse the home loops already designed (D2 and D5 notes).
