The home blood-pressure step loop
A reading taken at home becomes a proposed dose change, checked by rules, approved by a clinician and sent back in the patient's language.
1 Home
Any cheap cuff
Reading sent by Bluetooth
Phone photo
Of the cuff's screen, when there is no Bluetooth
Clip on the cuff
Arm position and stillness
Medicine check
"Did you take it every day?" in chat
→
2 Engine
aReading rules
Throw out bad readings; average the valid ones over 7 days
bTitrate or support
Medicine missed: support the patient, do not raise the dose
cProtocol state machine
Knows the current step, the next step and the safety branches
dProposal with reasons
Next step plus the facts that led to it
→
3 Clinic
Doctor or trained health worker
Sees the proposal, the readings and the reasons
Approves, changes or calls
One tap to approve. Urgent cases go to the top.
Safety branches
180/110 or more: refer. In pregnancy: no telmisartan or chlorthalidone.
→
4 Back home
New dose in the patient's language
Sent on a chat app, with what to watch for
Next check in 30 days
Readings continue and the loop runs again
Side effects reported in chat
Swelling or dizziness goes straight back to the clinic
