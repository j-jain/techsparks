Checks made for the note "SPARK Night 2026 - shared vitals station, idea check". One line per fact. OPENED = the page was read; SNIPPET = from a search summary only, page not opened (several pages answered 403).

## Facts

- D5-01-F01 | KGMU Lucknow study of Dozee-EWS (Yadav et al., Frontiers in Medical Technology, 29 Aug 2024): prospective single-centre cohort, two medicine wards with 72 beds, Oct 2022 to Jan 2023, 706 patients, 84,448 monitoring hours. Manual spot checks "conducted at intervals of 3-6 h" are the general-ward norm. HCP-to-patient ratio "1:36". 33 patients (5%) deteriorated (30 to ICU, 3 died). Sensitivity 67% at 24 h to 94% over the stay; specificity 19-42%; mean 16 h from first alert to deterioration. Five authors are employees of Turtle Shell Technologies (maker of Dozee), one is its consultant, and the study was partly funded by it. | https://frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fmedt.2024.1436034/full | OPENED
- D5-01-F02 | Same paper: "HCP spend 10% of their time on vital signs check and documentation", from a time-motion study of one junior resident over one 24-hour weekday; its Figure 3 shows vital checks at 7.6%. | https://frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fmedt.2024.1436034/full | OPENED
