### BIRAC funding routes for a student biotech or health team (verify on birac.nic.in; no open-call status confirmed)
- BIG (Biotechnology Ignition Grant): grant-in-aid up to INR 50 lakh for up to 18 months, ideation to proof of concept; Company/LLP must be incorporated no earlier than 5 years before the call launch; support only once per startup; individuals can apply but an incubator LoI/MoU is needed for final approval; not a research fellowship and cannot fund PhD/academic research; national calls at least twice a year (typically 1 Jan and 1 Jul), open ~30 days — https://www.birac.nic.in/webcontent/1761936564_big_new_guidelines_nov_2025.pdf (Nov 2025 guidelines, PDF unreadable; figures from search snippet) and older guidelines https://www.birac.nic.in/webcontent/1568612112_BIG_Guidelies.pdf. Next call timing in 2027: not confirmed.

- Other 2026 calls (snippet): DBT-BIRAC Bio-AI joint call and Product Commercialization Programme Fund, closing 30 Jun 2026 (passed) — https://innovationbridge.info/ibportal/content/dbt-birac-joint-call-bio-ai-birac-product-commercialization-program-pcp-fund-2026-india .
