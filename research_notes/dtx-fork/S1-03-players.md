# S1-03 Players: digital chronic-disease care competitor map (agent: Sonnet 5.5, 2026-11-13)

Tags: OPENED = page read. SNIPPET = search-result text only. THEIR CLAIM = company number. Depth scale 0-4 per brief D0. "Provisional" = depth judged from public marketing or snippets, not from a protocol document. Dates are as given by the source; "undated" if none.

## Digest

Facts that matter most (URLs in the Findings):
1. Omada FY2025: 886,000 members, revenue $260M (+53%), net loss $13M (2024: $47M), 150,000+ members supported on GLP-1s, 2,000+ clients (OPENED, 5 Mar 2026). KEY.
2. Hinge Health FY2025: revenue $587.9M (+51%), 782,890 members, GAAP net loss ~$528M from non-cash charges (SNIPPET of 10-K summaries).
3. Sugar.fit FY25 revenue Rs 66.5 cr (+77%), loss Rs 42 cr, ~$26M raised in total (OPENED, Entrackr 16 Jan 2026). KEY.
4. BeatO sells a glucometer from Rs 549 and a "doctor-led" GLP-1 programme; its HbA1c claim (-2.16%) is "a subset of 100 users who strictly adhered" (OPENED). KEY.
5. Twin Health India: three sensors plus care team over video; claims "94% achieve reversal in RCTs", "Audited data (IIT Madras)", no price listed (OPENED). Not stated who changes medicines. KEY.
6. Failures cluster on payment, not technology: Pear collected ~41% on 45,000 prescriptions (as reported) and sold assets for ~$6M; Teladoc wrote off $6.6B + $3B on Livongo in 2022.
7. Depth-4 products exist only abroad: Welldoc BlueStar (several FDA 510(k)s for insulin dosing; bolus advisor Aug 2023) and Hygieia d-Nav (FDA K181916, 4 Feb 2019, clinician-trained use only).
8. I found NO Indian company documenting protocol-driven dose or drug-step changes from home readings (depth 4). Absence is "not found in 30 searches", not proof.
9. HealthifyMe Rx: tirzepatide programme Rs 48,000 for 3 months (Inc42, 2025) or Rs 65,000 for 3 months (pricing page snippet); CGM add-on Rs 5,249/sensor.
10. Propeller Health: ResMed page says the company "has ceased operations" (SNIPPET, undated).
11. Public-sector hypertension: IHCI SIMPLE app, ~51 lakh hypertension patients (programme slides, SNIPPET); Medtronic LABS SPICE open-source platform for health workers. Both are facility or health-worker tools, not home-reading titration.
12. Sword agreed to buy Kaia for $285M (Jan 2026, SNIPPET): consolidation among global digital MSK players.

Surprises: (a) Sugar.fit's revenue (Rs 66.5 cr) already exceeds BeatO's most likely FY25 (Rs 53 cr, with a conflicting 28.5 cr on the same page). (b) Wellthy (a "prescribable app" pioneer) was reportedly acquired by TruDoc in Feb 2024, Fitterfly by PB Healthcare Services in Nov 2025 (both aggregator-only), so Indian DTx pure-plays are consolidating. (c) GLP-1 is where Indian cash-pay money now sits (Healthify, BeatO, Elevate Now Rs 18 cr seed Feb 2026).

Contradicts hypotheses: (1) H1: not tested here; no peer-reviewed head-to-head found in this note (NOT SEARCHED). Omada's own claim is about weight maintenance, not depth. (2) H4: the strongest-funded depth-4-like players (Twin) rest on outcome data and payer contracts, but Pear/Better had FDA clearance and clinical data and still failed on payment, so validation alone is not a moat. (3) H5: Lupin's Lyfe holds a CDSCO Class C software-as-medical-device licence (Apr 2024, SNIPPET), so a licensed Indian pathway for clinical software exists; I did not read what Lyfe adapts.

### 5. The gap (with what I searched)

1. Depth 4 in India (dose or therapy step changed from home readings with clinician sign-off). Searched: BeatO, Sugar.fit, Twin Health India, HealthifyMe, Elevate Now, Wellthy/Fitterfly/Phable, Lupin Lyfe, plus one query for "India remote insulin titration / drug titration app". Result: none documents a protocol. Twin, Elevate Now and BeatO/Healthify GLP-1 programmes plausibly do clinician-led titration but do not publish the rule. Label: "not documented", not "does not exist". Welldoc ran a pilot with Max Healthcare (SNIPPET, techemerge) and Welldoc has FDA insulin-dosing clearances abroad.
2. Hypertension at home. Searched: one query (India HTN home BP titration CHW). Found IHCI SIMPLE (clinic-based), a Kerala WhatsApp trial and Udupi mHealth cluster RCT protocol (self-management, not titration), m-Power Heart nurse-led CHC trial. No Indian company found selling home-BP-driven drug-step titration. BeatO lists BP monitors only. Twin lists hypertension. Not checked: Tricog/Omron KeeboHealth detail, Eka Care, Practo, Ekincare.
3. Small-town doctors / public clinics: only Medtronic LABS (SPICE) and IHCI SIMPLE found, both facility-based and health-worker-driven. Private apps found (BeatO, Sugar.fit, Healthify, Twin, Elevate Now) are direct-to-consumer, urban, Hindi/English, cash-pay (Twin hires Hindi health coaches).
4. Heart failure: Tricog + Omron (Nov 2025) remote HF monitoring is monitoring (depth 1 to 2 provisional). Kidney disease and anticoagulation (home INR): searched in one combined query; nothing found. Label "not found, search shallow".
5. Respiratory: Cipla CipAir (screening), Breathefree (reminders, tracking), Lupin JAI (education). All depth 1 to 2. Spirofy NOT CONFIRMED. No sensor-driven inhaler titration found; Propeller (global precedent) has ceased operations.
6. Evidence gap: of Indian players, only Twin-linked authors (PMID 39634180) and a Phable-linked study (Chawla et al., JMIR Diabetes 2022, SNIPPET) surfaced as peer-reviewed; BeatO/Sugar.fit/Healthify outcome figures are company claims.
